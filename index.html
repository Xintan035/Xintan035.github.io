My name is Xin Tan, and I am an aspiring accounting professional with an academic background in economics from the University of California, Riverside. Studying economics helped me develop an understanding of how businesses operate, how financial decisions are made, and how organizations respond to changes in the economy. Throughout my education, I became increasingly interested in the connection between economic concepts and the everyday financial activities that support a successful business. I appreciate how accounting turns financial information into organized records that help people understand performance, manage resources, and make informed decisions. My academic experience encouraged me to approach problems carefully, examine information from different perspectives, and consider both individual details and their broader implications.

These habits have shaped my interest in building a career that combines analytical thinking with practical responsibility. I am particularly drawn to accounting because it requires consistency, accountability, and a willingness to investigate discrepancies rather than overlook them. I believe accurate financial information provides an essential foundation for effective business operations, regardless of an organization’s size or industry. My education at UCR also strengthened my ability to manage competing priorities, work independently, and communicate ideas clearly. As I continue developing professionally, I hope to apply this foundation to accounting work that supports dependable reporting and thoughtful financial management. I view my economics background as a valuable starting point for understanding not only the numbers within financial records, but also the business activities and decisions those numbers represent in the context of an organization’s overall financial position.

In addition to my academic background, I have worked in accounting assistant positions, gaining exposure to the daily responsibilities of supporting accounting teams and financial operations. These experiences helped me better understand the importance of organized information, accurate records, and timely completion of assigned work. Working in a supporting role taught me that even small tasks contribute to a larger process, and that attention to detail matters at every stage. I approach my responsibilities with patience and a willingness to ask questions when clarification is needed. Rather than assuming that a task is complete simply because the information has been entered, I value reviewing work and understanding its purpose.
